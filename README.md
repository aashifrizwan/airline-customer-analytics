# Airline Customer Analytics

## About This Project

This project explores the booking behaviour of customers at a fictional airline.

The aim is to understand who the airline’s customers are, how they book and what influences the revenue generated from each booking. The analysis will eventually turn these findings into practical recommendations for the airline’s commercial and marketing teams.

## Business Problem

The airline collects booking and customer data but does not yet have a clear view of:

- Which customer groups generate the most revenue
- Which routes and booking channels perform best
- How far in advance customers usually book
- What influences customers to purchase additional services
- Which customers are most likely to make another booking

## Questions to Answer

1. Which routes generate the most bookings and revenue?
2. Which booking channels perform best?
3. How does booking lead time affect fares and customer behaviour?
4. Which customer segments purchase the most ancillary services?
5. What factors are associated with repeat bookings?

## Planned Approach

The project will include:

- Data cleaning and preparation
- Exploratory data analysis
- Customer segmentation
- Booking and revenue analysis
- Data visualisation
- Commercial recommendations
